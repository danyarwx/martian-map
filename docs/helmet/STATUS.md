## Questions for the human
- Contract change (Sasha + Claude, 14 Nov, FIXLIST A2): `Detection.level?: 'hazard' | 'small'` (rocks only; absent = hazard), `Detection.outline?: number[]` (closed polygon in the left image, x0, y0, x1, y1, …) and `Detection.groundZ?` (the CV's ground height under the detection). All optional. Small rocks never become `HAZARD.rock` cells. `detectObstacles` takes the frame's disparity map as a sixth argument
- Danya (A2): the CV cameras are now 384 × 288 with 60 disparities (depth from 1.4 m instead of 1.1 m). Worker time rose from 21 ms to 55 ms per frame (budget 60), about 5 Hz. A rock measured 0.26–0.30 m counts as a hazard (`hazardMarginM` 0.04: stereo reads small rocks a few cm low). OK, or prefer 256 × 192 (66 % recall, 22 ms)?
- Danya: three things changed how the demo behaves and are yours to veto. (1) A **simulated sand look** was added to the terrain where the orbital sand mask says sand (8 % darker, less red, ripples), because the classifier could not learn sand from the plain ortho (F1 0.60); it is labelled simulated in SOURCES.md. (3) `#helmet-eval` ships in the production build as a lazy 45 kB chunk
- Contract change (new owner, 14 Nov): `Pose.rollDeg?` (optional; positive = camera's right side lower). Only `cv/points.ts` `cameraAxes` reads it; the helmet's own cameras never set it. Needed for the real rover photo, whose frame is rolled −11.5°
